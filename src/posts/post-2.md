Madrid is ideal for travellers who want atmosphere without constant spending. Some of our favourite hours here cost almost nothing: a bakery coffee at the bar, wandering through Lavapies, then stretching the afternoon in a shaded plaza.

The trick is to avoid treating every meal like an event. Split your day between one memorable dish and a series of cheap, casual stops. The city is built for that rhythm.

For us, Madrid has also been one of the easier places to settle into as a queer couple. The city feels social instead of performative, and that changes how comfortable daily life becomes.
