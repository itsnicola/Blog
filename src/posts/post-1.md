Istanbul rewards anyone willing to trade polished tourist plans for ferries, markets, and cheap, excellent meals. Our best days were the ones with almost no agenda: simit for breakfast, long walks between neighbourhoods, and one public transport card doing most of the heavy lifting.

The city can feel enormous at first, so our budget rule was simple: pick one area in the morning, one in the evening, and let the water link everything else. That kept costs low and made the days feel less frantic.

If you are travelling as a queer couple, we found it easier to relax in busier neighbourhoods where being anonymous felt natural. Istanbul is vibrant and generous, but reading the room matters, especially at night.
